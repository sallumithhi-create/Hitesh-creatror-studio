📝 Quick title tips

A useful checklist before publishing.

  • Put the main topic near the beginning.
  • Make the promise clear and honest.
  • Avoid misleading clickbait.
  • Use a clear face or subject in the thumbnail.

📈 Before you publish

A simple upload checklist.

  • Check spelling in title and description.
  • Add only relevant keywords and hashtags.
  • Choose the correct audience setting.
  • Review the thumbnail on mobile size.
